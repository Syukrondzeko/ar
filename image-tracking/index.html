🖼️ Image Tracking

Point the camera at a picture and it comes to life in 3D: the Eiffel Tower or the Leaning Tower of Pisa.

1. Ready-made pictures

Open any of these on another screen (or print it), then start. Tap a picture to open it full size.

2. Your own picture

A book cover, poster, photo, packaging… The page prepares it for tracking (10–60 seconds). A dancing robot appears on it.

  • ✅ Good pictures: lots of detail, sharp edges, strong contrast (posters, book covers, photos with texture).
  • ❌ Bad pictures: plain colors, simple logos, repeating patterns, very dark images.
  • Keep the picture flat and well lit, and show the whole picture to the camera.
